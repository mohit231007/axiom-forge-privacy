Privacy and support

Effective 4 October 2026. This policy covers Axiom Forge Android version 1.3.0, provided by Mohit Bhatnagar under The Algosphere brand.

Your learning data

Your notes, interview answers, self-scores, completed topics, saved experiments and display preferences are stored locally on your device. The Android app does not require an account, include ads or analytics, or send this learning data to our servers. It does not include a hosted AI coach.

Files and sharing

You choose which text notes or backup files to import using the system file picker. Export creates a temporary file in the app cache and opens Android’s share sheet. Data leaves the app only when you choose a recipient or destination. That destination’s privacy terms then apply. Temporary exports are removed at the next launch and when you clear device data in the app.

Retention and deletion

Use My progress → Clear device data to remove learning records and temporary exports. Uninstalling or clearing the app’s storage removes its local data. Android cloud backup is disabled for this release. Backups you saved outside the app remain under your control and must be deleted separately. Export important progress before uninstalling.

External links

Opening research references or contacting support may use your browser or email application. Those services can process network information according to their own policies. The app does not control those services.

Feedback and support

Axiom Forge includes an optional Send feedback action. On Android it opens the system share sheet with a feedback text file; on the web it opens your email application. Nothing is sent automatically. If you include context, the feedback contains only the current screen, app version, view setting and platform. Email mbrox18@gmail.com for feedback, support or privacy questions. If you email us, your address and the information you provide are processed to respond. Do not send passwords, payment details or sensitive private notes. Request deletion of support correspondence at the same address; we retain it only as needed to resolve the request or meet applicable obligations.

Audience and changes

Axiom Forge is designed for adult learners and professionals. We do not knowingly collect children’s personal information. We will update this policy when the Android app’s data practices change.

Web and local development editions

The browser edition stores progress in browser storage. Its hosting provider may process access logs or authentication information. The separate Python development edition can optionally send selected passages and a query to a model provider after an explicit request. That coach is not included in the Android release covered above.

Third-party software notices are included in the application.
